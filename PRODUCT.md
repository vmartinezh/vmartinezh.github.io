# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/vanilla JS, no build step, published on GitHub Pages from the repo `vmartinezh/vmartinezh.github.io` (URL: https://vmartinezh.github.io). Delegated choice driven by the GitHub Pages target.

## Users

Small business owners and entrepreneurs (pymes, emprendimientos) in Chile, mostly reached through Instagram stories, WhatsApp and word of mouth from friends. They arrive on a phone, want to know quickly what MH Digital Studio does, see real work, get a sense of price, and write on WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

Presentation site for MH Digital Studio, a personal tech-services studio run by Vicente Martínez. Success = a visitor understands the services and sends a WhatsApp message or email asking for a quote.

## Positioning

Not "just a web freelancer": a small studio of tech solutions for pymes, run by someone with an IT-support background (Jefe de Soporte TI). One contact covers the website, the computers, the email setup and the tech decisions of a small business.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Service lines (confirmed in the source conversation):
1. Desarrollo web — landing pages, sitios corporativos (WordPress or HTML), catálogo, formulario + WhatsApp, optimización básica.
2. Mantención y reparación de equipos — computadores lentos o sucios, limpieza, optimización, formateo.
3. Consultoría TI — configuración inicial de correos corporativos, asesoría en compra de equipos, estructuración de la tecnología de la empresa.
4. Seguridad básica para empresas — revisión de seguridad, antivirus, respaldos, protección de correos, revisión de accesos (suggested line, user agreed to an "umbrella for everything digital").

Pricing: NO prices on the site (user decision 2026-10-11: "mejor que coticen"; showing prices could scare clients away). Every service routes to a WhatsApp quote. Internal reference figures (landing $250.000, etc.) exist but stay off the site.

Working terms the user uses: 50% al inicio / 50% contra entrega, 2 rondas de revisiones, plazos definidos.

Contact data (WhatsApp, email, Instagram): NOT provided yet; site ships with clearly marked placeholders in one config spot.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: MH Digital Studio (MH = Martínez Higuera). Tagline candidates from the conversation: "Soluciones web para empresas que quieren crecer online", "Desarrollo web profesional y soluciones digitales".
- Logo direction already chosen: bold "MH" in electric-blue gradient on dark navy, "DIGITAL STUDIO" smaller in white sans-serif. Favicon: "MH" initials, blue gradient on dark navy square.
- Voice: Chilean Spanish, cercano, honesto, profesional sin ser corporativo frío; "tú".

## Product Principles

1. Real work over claims: show the live projects.
2. Every section leads to WhatsApp.
3. Quote-first: no published prices; every service ends in a WhatsApp quote.
4. Broad umbrella, one trusted contact.
